About

Building at the intersection of
engineering & product.

I'm Pardeep Dhingra, a Full Stack Engineer and Solution Architect with over a decade of experience building production systems at scale. Currently working at Upflowy, where I design and architect solutions that help businesses optimize their growth funnels.

My approach is architecture-first — I believe great software starts with clear thinking about system design, data flow, and scalability before a single line of code is written. I work across the full stack, from cloud infrastructure (AWS, CDK) to frontend interfaces (React, TypeScript).

When I'm not building products, I'm writing about what I learn — sharing practical insights on engineering, architecture patterns, and the evolving tech landscape.
